THREE-PHASE PROGRESSION
PHASE 01

Foundation

JUNE 15 TO JULY 13

Movement, S&C, hill, beach are primary. On-court begins but the movement foundation leads.

  • Movement bandwidth: dowel work, trail legs, narrow to wide, delays, gallops, body fakes
  • Jump rope and hit band layering on grooved patterns
  • Acceleration mechanics: mini hurdles, ground contact, sprint capacity
  • Plyometric development: ankling, A and B skips, bounding, jump tucks
  • Hill work and beach work with Aquavest. Deceleration focus
  • Daily hip mobility
  • Weight room and S&C
PHASE 02

On-Court Integration

JULY 14 TO AUGUST 3

Ball on the court. Deep distance calibration ramps up. Live work 3x per week. Court days carry more weight.

  • Deep distance calibration at game speed. Airspace from three. Aquavest and oversized ball
  • W collections. Abrupt stop and go into the shot or downhill
  • Variable fade patterns. Variable catch locations
  • High release point exploration off DHO
  • Ball manipulation: RT and RL, maintain the dribble under pressure, vision up
  • Constraint layering: strobes, oversized ball, silent players, blockers, reduced rim
  • Post-up development and DHO action preparation
  • Passing and vision: read the help, two targets, decide off what you see
PHASE 03

Live Play and Game Prep

AUGUST 4 TO SEPTEMBER 1

Live play with constraints, gradual constraint removal, pre-season simulation. Live and court are primary, movement is maintained.

  • Live play with constraints: 3v3 with strobes, oversized ball, blockers
  • Gradual constraint removal to feel the contrast
  • Competitive live sessions with hungry players
  • Read and react under stress: zero to three dribble, silent player
  • Conditioning through live play
  • Weekly film review and adjustment
  • Pre-season simulation. Game 7 level effort
